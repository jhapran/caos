Product Requirements Document (PRD)
AI-Native Practice, Compliance & Client Operating System for
Chartered Accountant Firms
Working Product Name: CAOS — CA Operating System
Alternative names: FinPilot CA, PracticeAI, CAFlow, ComplyOS, LedgerPilot
Version: 0.1 — Product Discovery / MVP
Primary Market: Indian Chartered Accountant firms
Initial Target Segment: CA firms with 5–50 employees and approximately 100–2,000 client entities




1. Product Vision
Build an AI-native operating system for Chartered Accountant firms that brings together:


      • clients
      • entities
      • compliance
      • documents
      • communication
      • work allocation
      • review
      • financial information
      • audit evidence
      • notices
      • billing
      • advisory opportunities

into one intelligent workspace.


The product should become the system of record and system of action for a CA practice.


It should not initially try to replace established products such as:


      • Tally
      • Zoho Books
      • ClearTax
      • Winman
      • KDK
      • CompuTax

Instead, it should connect these systems and manage the workflow between them.

2. Product Mission
Help CA firms spend less time on:


     • chasing clients
     • searching for documents
     • updating Excel trackers
     • assigning recurring tasks
     • asking for information already provided
     • checking which return is pending
     • consolidating information from multiple systems
     • preparing status reports
     • identifying missed deadlines
     • checking work manually across hundreds of clients

and spend more time on:


     • review
     • advisory
     • client relationships
     • risk management
     • higher-value professional services.




3. Core Product Promise
       Know what is due, what is missing, who is responsible, and what needs attention —
       across every client.


Secondary promise:


       Request information once. Use it everywhere.




4. The Core Problem
A typical CA firm may use:


     • Tally for accounting
     • Excel for compliance tracking
     • tax software for income-tax returns
     • GST software
     • MCA portal
     • Income Tax portal

     • email
     • Google Drive/OneDrive/local folders for documents
     • Word for engagement letters
     • separate billing software
     • Excel for staff allocation
     • Excel for client profitability

The individual applications may work well.


The problem is that information is fragmented.


Consider one client:


ABC Pvt Ltd

     • Tally
     • GST software

There is usually no unified intelligence layer connecting these systems.




5. Product Strategy
The application should be designed around four layers.


Layer 1 — Client Intelligence
Build a comprehensive client/entity knowledge graph.


Layer 2 — Workflow Intelligence
Understand:


     • what needs to happen




                                                      3
      • when
      • who should do it
      • what information is required
      • what is blocking completion


Layer 3 — Professional AI
Use AI for:


      • document extraction
      • classification
      • reconciliation
      • anomaly identification
      • summarisation
      • drafting
      • research
      • workflow automation

but retain CA review and approval.


Layer 4 — Advisory Intelligence
Use accumulated financial and compliance information to identify:


      • financial risks
      • business issues
      • improvement opportunities
      • additional professional services.

11. Primary Jobs To Be Done
JTBD 1
When a compliance deadline approaches, I want to know which clients are ready and which are not so I can
focus my team on exceptions.


JTBD 2
When a client's documents arrive through email, portal or WhatsApp, I want them automatically identified,
stored and attached to the correct client.


JTBD 3
When documents are missing, I want the software to follow up automatically.


JTBD 4
When I open a client, I want to see everything related to that client.


JTBD 5
When reviewing work, I want the preparation, evidence, calculations and reviewer comments together.


JTBD 6
When a partner asks about firm status, I want instant answers instead of preparing an Excel report.
